What our systems do for your line

Four situations every customer asks about. Pick one and follow the data between your company, our systems and the shop floor.

Stage 1 · Warehouse and laser cutting

A sheet becomes a blank

Sheets and profiles go from the warehouse straight to the laser. Each blank gets an order number that stays with it to the end of the hall.

Made here
  • Blanks for bus side flaps
  • Blanks for facade cassettes
  • Ceiling frame profiles cut to length
Data capturedMaterial batch scanned on receipt. Nesting comes straight from the CAD/CAM system.
Stage 2 · Bending and CNC milling

The flat blank takes shape

The press brake folds the sheet and the CNC centre mills holes, pockets and weld-prep edges.

Made here
  • Facade cassettes with irregular geometry
  • Driver’s desk housings
  • Wheel arches and body panels
Data capturedEach job is reported to MES at the machine: operator, program, pieces, time.
Stage 3 · Welding

Robots join the frame

Two robots weld the structure in a single fixture. Critical welds are tested before the part moves on.

  • EN 15085 CL 1
  • ISO 3834-2
Made here
  • Gangway frames
  • Tanks for buses
  • Control cabinet structures
Data capturedWelding parameters are logged per seam, with the welder’s ID and procedure number.
Stage 4 · Bonding and powder coating

This is where colour goes on

Panels are bonded layer by layer, then everything passes through the coating tunnel. It goes in silver and comes out finished.

  • EN 17460 A1
Made here
  • Bonded ceiling panels with acoustic insulation
  • Powder coating on every part
Data capturedAdhesive batch and cure time per panel. Oven temperature curve per load.
Stage 5 · Assembly and quality control

Parts become a module

Frame, insulation, panel and lighting come together as one module. A 3D scanner checks it against the customer’s CAD model.

Made here
  • Complete ceiling modules with lighting
  • A measurement report with every delivery
Data capturedThe 3D scan report is stored against the part’s code and sent with the delivery.
